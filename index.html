1Research

Ongoing work supervised by Danil Sagunov. Both projects began at LIPS 2026, the summer research programme of MIPT's Laboratory of Combinatorial and Geometric Structures, and have continued since.

2026 – presentOngoing
Joint work

Diverse matroid bases and spanning trees

  • Proved that finding three matroid bases maximizing the minimum pairwise distance is solvable in polynomial time, using O(r) oracle calls, and in O(n) time for spanning trees.
  • Proved the same for four diverse spanning trees, and showed that the optimal minimum distance is determined by the optimal total distance.
  • Extended the four-basis result to general matroids.

Manuscript in preparation. Presented at the LIPS 2026 conference, MIPT.

2026 – presentOngoing
Joint work

Diverse maximum cuts in planar graphs

  • Characterized exactly when a graph has three maximum cuts at the largest possible pairwise distance, reducing a search over triples to a search over pairs.
  • For planar graphs, showed this happens exactly when the max-cut bound 3·OPT ≥ 2m is tight, which makes this case of an open problem decidable and constructive in polynomial time.
  • Checked the characterization by exhaustive computation on more than 23,000 graphs.

Manuscript in preparation. Presented at the LIPS 2026 conference, MIPT.

Cloud point-of-sale system

A multi-store point-of-sale platform for retail businesses, covering checkout, inventory, purchasing, returns, staff and reporting.

  • Checkout with split payments, customer lookup and receipts; stock transfers between stores run as single database transactions.

  • Role-based access with per-user permission overrides, refresh-token rotation with reuse detection, and an audit log of every change.
